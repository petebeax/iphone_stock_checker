iPhone Watchlist

Add phones, choose valid Apple configurations, and route each restock email to its own recipient.

1. Unlock your watchlist

Your list is encrypted before being committed to your public repository. Your passphrase and GitHub token are kept in this browser tab only and are never saved by this page.

2. Manage alerts

All models, storage and colours are loaded from the product catalogue generated by your checker.

How it works

After you save, the next cron-triggered GitHub Actions run reads secure_watchlist.json and uses your encrypted alerts. Existing WATCHLIST_JSON is a fallback only until the first encrypted save. Your email SMTP settings and cron job stay unchanged.

This page is publicly viewable, but editing requires your GitHub token, and reading saved alerts requires your passphrase. It is not a separate login service. Do not share either credential.
